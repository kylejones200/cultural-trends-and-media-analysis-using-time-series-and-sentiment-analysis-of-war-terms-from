# Chronotopes of Culture: Forecasting Aesthetic and Socioeconomic Futures

## Abstract
This whitepaper sketches an interdisciplinary framework for anticipating cultural futures by combining quantitative time-series forecasting with narrative interpretation. Drawing on commodities, cultural production data, and macroeconomic indicators, the project orchestrates scenario-based storytelling aimed at scholars, policy makers, and public audiences.

## Research Questions
1. How do economic safe-haven signals (e.g., gold, energy infrastructure) intersect with cultural production metrics (film releases, book sales) over time?
2. Can machine learning and neural forecasts illuminate divergent cultural trajectories under specific macro scenarios (inflation spikes, climate events)?
3. What storytelling strategies best translate numerical forecasts into accessible cultural narratives?

## Methods Overview
- **Data assemblage:** curated via `data_catalog.yaml`, harmonized with `build_dataset.py`.
- **Modeling pipeline:** leverages StatsForecast, NeuralForecast, and custom ensemble templates from the broader repository.
- **Scenario analysis:** runs multiple “futures” varying macro covariates.
- **Narrative synthesis:** automated briefs (`evaluate_narratives.py`) followed by human-curated essays and dashboards.

## Deliverables
- Peer-reviewed article targeting journals such as *Journal of Cultural Analytics* or *New Media & Society*.
- Interactive dashboard presenting “chronoscopes” of cultural futures.
- Teaching module for graduate seminars on temporal methods in digital humanities.
- Public-facing essay or exhibit highlighting speculative cultural trajectories.
